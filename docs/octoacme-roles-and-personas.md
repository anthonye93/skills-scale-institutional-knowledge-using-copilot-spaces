# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and feature prioritization
- Work with **Project Managers** on estimation and scheduling
- Coordinate with **QA Lead** on test strategy and quality standards
- Receive technical direction from **Technical Architect** on design decisions
- Support **Scrum Master** in removing impediments and following Definition of Done
- Provide implementation feedback to **Business Analyst** on feasibility and technical constraints

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **Project Managers** on timeline and release planning
- Work with **Developers** to define clear acceptance criteria
- Align with **Sponsor** on business priorities and strategic fit
- Collaborate with **Business Analyst** to refine requirements and user stories
- Incorporate feedback from **QA Lead** on quality standards and release readiness
- Review technical trade-offs with **Technical Architect** on feasibility

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with **Developers** on estimation, scheduling, and capacity planning
- Coordinate with **Product Manager** on backlog prioritization and scope management
- Partner with **Scrum Master** on sprint planning and team ceremonies
- Report to **Sponsor** on progress, risks, and business impact
- Collaborate with **Release Manager** on release coordination and deployment scheduling
- Ensure **Business Analyst** has support for requirements gathering activities

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile practices and remove impediments that block team progress. They coach the team on agile methodologies, ensure adherence to the Definition of Done, and foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and blockers that hinder team progress
- Coach team members on agile practices and principles
- Ensure the team follows the Definition of Done and workflow standards
- Track and communicate team velocity and burndown metrics
- Foster psychological safety and encourage feedback

### Goals
- Enable high-performing, self-organizing teams
- Maintain consistent sprint velocity and predictable delivery
- Create a culture of continuous improvement and shared ownership
- Reduce cycle time and unplanned work through better practices

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups with the team
- Sprint ceremonies (planning, review, retrospective)
- One-on-one coaching conversations
- Escalation of cross-team impediments

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **Project Managers** on sprint scheduling and resource coordination
- Support **Developers** in identifying and removing blockers
- Work with **Product Manager** to ensure backlog is refined and ready for sprint planning
- Facilitate collaboration between **QA Lead** and developers on acceptance criteria
- Report metrics and team health to **Project Manager** for program-level visibility
- Support **Technical Architect** in communicating technical guidance to the team

---

## Quality Assurance / QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and execute comprehensive testing strategies to ensure products meet quality standards and acceptance criteria. They own quality metrics, manage defect lifecycle, and collaborate with developers to build quality into the development process.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, test plans, and acceptance criteria validation approach
- Lead manual and automated testing efforts
- Own quality metrics, defect management, and root cause analysis
- Collaborate with developers on test coverage and quality standards
- Validate release readiness and conduct final QA sign-off
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Document known issues and limitations

### Goals
- Ensure products meet or exceed quality standards before release
- Reduce defects in production through proactive testing and automation
- Build quality into the development process, not just at the end
- Enable fast, reliable releases with confidence

### Typical Communication
- Daily coordination with developers on defects and test results
- Sprint planning to estimate testing effort
- Status reports on quality metrics and test coverage
- Release readiness reviews with Project Manager and Release Manager

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Developers** on unit test coverage, integration testing, and defect resolution
- Work with **Product Manager** to validate acceptance criteria and business requirements
- Partner with **Project Manager** on test scheduling and quality gate decisions
- Support **Scrum Master** in ensuring quality is part of the Definition of Done
- Coordinate with **Release Manager** on pre-release testing and smoke test execution
- Provide quality feedback to **Technical Architect** on architectural decisions

---

## Technical Architect / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide strategic technical direction and design guidance for projects. They review architectural decisions, manage technical debt, mentor developers, and ensure best practices are followed across the team.

### Responsibilities
- Provide technical direction and design guidance for features and systems
- Review architectural decisions and trade-offs with development team
- Identify and manage technical debt and risk
- Mentor developers and ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices
- Coordinate with other technical teams on integrations and dependencies
- Conduct design reviews and code quality assessments
- Recommend tooling, frameworks, and architectural patterns

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable, and secure systems
- Reduce technical risk and debt over time
- Enable team efficiency through good architectural decisions
- Foster knowledge sharing and continuous technical growth

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- One-on-one mentoring with developers
- Integration planning with other technical teams
- Risk assessment and mitigation planning

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Guide **Developers** on technical design and implementation best practices
- Advise **Product Manager** on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Inform **Project Manager** on technical risks and dependencies
- Support **QA Lead** in designing test strategies aligned with architecture
- Coordinate with **Release Manager** on technical deployment considerations
- Advise **Business Analyst** on technical constraints affecting requirements

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors are business leaders who provide strategic oversight, business justification, and executive decision-making authority. They ensure projects align with organizational strategy, remove high-level blockers, and own business outcomes and success metrics.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business justification and secure funding/approval for projects
- Serve as escalation point for business-impacting decisions
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy and business objectives
- Review progress against business outcomes and success metrics
- Remove high-level blockers and organizational constraints
- Communicate business impact to other executives
- Own business risks and mitigation strategies

### Goals
- Maximize return on investment for project initiatives
- Ensure strategic alignment and business value realization
- Remove organizational barriers to successful delivery
- Enable business-driven prioritization of cross-team dependencies

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based business reviews
- Escalation meetings for high-impact decisions
- Executive status summaries and business metrics
- Strategic planning and roadmap discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Review progress with **Project Manager** and **Product Manager** through regular business reviews
- Make resource and priority trade-off decisions affecting **Developers** and other teams
- Work with **Product Manager** on strategic business outcomes and success metrics
- Escalate blockers from **Project Manager** to organizational leadership
- Receive technical risk summaries from **Technical Architect** for business impact assessment
- Review release milestones with **Release Manager** for business announcements

---

## Business Analyst / Requirements Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams. They elicit, document, and clarify requirements to ensure solutions accurately address business needs while being technically feasible.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document stakeholder requirements and business processes
- Clarify acceptance criteria with Product Manager and development team
- Conduct user research and document user stories
- Validate solutions against business requirements
- Document business workflows and decision logic
- Identify and communicate requirements risks and gaps
- Support requirements traceability and change management

### Goals
- Ensure clear understanding of business needs across the team
- Reduce rework caused by misunderstood or incomplete requirements
- Enable faster, more accurate delivery aligned with business intent
- Build shared understanding between business and technical teams

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- User story refinement with Product Manager
- Acceptance criteria clarification with Developers
- Requirements documentation and traceability updates

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with **Product Manager** to refine backlog and prioritize features based on business value
- Collaborate with **Developers** to clarify technical feasibility and refine acceptance criteria
- Support **QA Lead** in validating that solutions meet business requirements
- Provide requirements clarity to **Project Manager** for estimation and planning
- Advise **Technical Architect** on business constraints and integration requirements
- Support **Scrum Master** in sprint planning by ensuring stories are clear and ready for development

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release planning and execution to ensure smooth, predictable deployments to production. They manage release documentation, verify deployment readiness, and communicate releases to stakeholders and support teams.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release planning and scheduling across projects
- Manage release notes and deployment documentation
- Verify all deployment checklists are complete and approved
- Coordinate with DevOps on deployment execution and timing
- Conduct post-deployment verification and smoke tests
- Manage rollback procedures and incident communication
- Maintain release history and lessons learned

### Goals
- Enable reliable, repeatable deployment processes
- Reduce release-related incidents and rollbacks
- Provide clear visibility into what's being deployed and when
- Minimize deployment risk and customer impact

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and coordination calls
- Deployment checklists and status updates
- Release notes and stakeholder announcements
- Post-deployment verification reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Coordinate with **Project Manager** on release scheduling and readiness
- Review **QA Lead** sign-off and smoke test results before proceeding
- Obtain approval from **Product Manager** on release content and business impact
- Communicate with **Developers** on deployment requirements and rollback procedures
- Work with **Technical Architect** on deployment architecture and technical considerations
- Report deployment status to **Sponsor** for business announcements
- Coordinate with DevOps teams on infrastructure and deployment automation

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When running projects, clarify which roles are present on your team and reference these definitions to establish clear expectations.
